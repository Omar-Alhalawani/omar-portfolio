About

I’m Omar, a Computer Systems Engineering student at Carleton University interested in digital logic, ASIC verification, embedded systems, processors, and the tools engineers use to build and validate them.

I enjoy projects where software has to interact with something real - RTL, sensors, microcontrollers, laboratory instrumentation, or hardware interfaces. My work at Ciena introduced me to production ASIC verification methodology and strengthened my interest in semiconductor engineering.
